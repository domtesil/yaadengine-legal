YaadEngine — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026
Publisher: Dom Tesilbirth D Shira ("I", "me")
Contact: domtesil@gmail.com

YaadEngine is a flashcard app for studying with spaced repetition. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • YaadEngine does not collect your personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • Everything you create in the app stays on your own device. I do not receive it, and I have no servers that store it.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • I never sell or share your data, because I do not have it.

What the app stores on your device

When you use YaadEngine, it saves the following only inside the app's private storage on your phone:

  • the folders, decks and cards you create or import, including any pictures you add to cards;
  • your study progress and review history, which the app uses to schedule your reviews and to show your statistics;
  • your settings, such as the theme, the daily reminder time and which folders are open.

This information is not sent to me or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings, it is deleted from your device.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, and alarms at an exact time: used only to show the study reminders you switch on, at the time you choose. The app can restart those reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: used by notifications.

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or general file storage. When you add a picture, the app opens your phone's own camera or photo picker, and the app receives only the picture you choose. When you import a file, the app receives only the file you pick.

Files you import and export

You can import cards from files (CSV, Anki, or YaadEngine files) and export your decks and folders to a YaadEngine file. This happens only when you choose to. An exported file goes where you send or save it, for example to another app, to your cloud storage, or to a friend. I do not receive it.

Backups

Your phone may include the app's data in its normal Android backup to your Google account, depending on your device settings. That backup is managed by you and by Google, under Google's own policies. I do not have access to it.

Links to other services

The Settings screen has two links that open in your web browser:

  • Help & Feedback opens a form hosted by Jotform.
  • Suggest a Feature opens a feedback board hosted by Featurebase.

If you choose to use them, whatever you type there is handled by those services under their own privacy policies, not by the app. Please do not include sensitive personal information when you write to me.

Children

YaadEngine does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Your choices

Because your information stays on your device, you are in control of it. You can edit or delete any card, deck or folder in the app, export your data, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the effective date above, before the change reaches you.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please email domtesil@gmail.com.
