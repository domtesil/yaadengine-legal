YaadEngine — Changes to the Privacy Policy

This page lists every change to the YaadEngine Privacy Policy, newest first, with the date it took effect. The policy itself always shows the latest version.

19 October 2026

  • The optional Google Drive backup is described. The policy explains what is copied, where it goes (a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only YaadEngine can use), when it happens, and how to stop it or delete it.
  • Automatic backup at a time you choose. The optional automatic backup starts at the time of day you set in Settings, also while the app is closed, and only if something changed. While it runs, a quiet notification shows its progress.
  • New permissions explained. The app can use Android's background-work system so that the automatic backup can start at the chosen time. It does nothing until you have signed in and switched Auto-backup on.
  • Export to Anki is described.

15 October 2026

  • The first version of the app was released to testers with this Privacy Policy.
